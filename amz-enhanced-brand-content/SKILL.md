# Premium A+ and Brand Story

Standard A+ Content makes the product argument. Premium A+ and the Brand Story module
do something different. they build the brand. This skill plans the premium tier and
the cross-catalog Brand Story so a shopper sees a brand, not a one-off product.

## When to use this

- A brand qualifies for Premium A+ and is still publishing standard modules.
- A seller wants to set up the Brand Story carousel that runs across all listings.
- A product needs A+ video and the seller does not know which module or what to show.
- A catalog feels like unrelated products and the brand needs a connective layer.

## The two premium layers

### Layer 1. The Brand Story module

The Brand Story is a carousel that appears on **every listing in the brand**, just
above A+ Content. It is set once and shows everywhere. Its job is not to sell this
product. it is to answer "who is this brand and why does it exist", and to cross-sell
the rest of the catalog through its product-card slides.

Build it with three beats:
1. **The why.** One slide, one sentence. The reason the brand exists.
2. **The proof.** The credential, the standard, or the origin that makes the why real.
3. **The catalog.** Product-card slides that move the shopper to other brand items.

Set the Brand Story once. it then lifts every listing.

### Layer 2. Premium A+ modules

Premium A+ unlocks richer modules: full-width video, image carousels, interactive
hotspots, navigation carousels, larger comparison tables, and a Q&A module. The
upgrade is real estate and interactivity, not a different message.

Apply the A+ Conversion Ladder from amz-a-plus-content, then upgrade specific rungs
where interactivity answers the buyer question better:

- **Proof rung.** Replace a static image with video showing the product working.
- **Detail rung.** Use interactive hotspots on one hero image instead of four flat
  feature panels.
- **Compare rung.** Use the larger premium comparison table.
- **Close rung.** Use the Q&A module to kill the last specific objections in the
  buyer's own words.

A premium module earns its slot only when interactivity answers the question better
than a static module would. video for the sake of video is wasted budget.

## Step by step

1. **Confirm eligibility.** Premium A+ is invitation or threshold based and needs
   Brand Registry. Confirm before planning premium-only modules.

2. **Plan the Brand Story first.** It runs across every listing in the brand, so
   one asset lifts the whole catalog. Write the why, the proof, and the catalog slides.

3. **Audit each key listing's A+** against the Conversion Ladder.

4. **Upgrade rungs to premium** only where interactivity wins: video for Proof,
   hotspots for Detail, premium table for Compare, Q&A for Close.

5. **Brief the video.** Hook in the first 3 seconds, show the product solving the
   problem, end on the brand. Keep it short and legible without sound.

6. **Run the quality check**, then deliver.

## Worked example

A skincare brand, premium eligible.

Brand Story: slide 1 "Clean formulas, nothing hidden". slide 2 the dermatologist
test and the ingredient standard. catalog slides for the cleanser, serum, and moisturizer.

Premium upgrades on the serum listing: Proof rung becomes a 15-second video of the
texture and absorption. Close rung becomes a Q&A module answering the three
objections that recur in reviews. Detail rung stays static, hotspots would add
nothing there.

## Quality check

- Premium eligibility is confirmed before premium-only modules are planned.
- The Brand Story is planned first, because it lifts the entire catalog.
- The Brand Story includes catalog product-card slides for cross-sell.
- Premium modules are used only where interactivity beats a static module.
- Any video is briefed to be legible without sound and to hook in 3 seconds.

## Common mistakes

- **Skipping the Brand Story.** The single A+ asset that lifts every listing in the
  brand, left unset.
- **Video for its own sake.** A premium video that just shows the product rotating
  adds nothing a static image did not.
- **Treating Premium as a redesign.** The message does not change. the real estate
  and interactivity do.
- **A Brand Story with no catalog slides.** Missing the free cross-sell across every
  listing in the brand.
