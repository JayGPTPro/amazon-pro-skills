# Creator Connections Brief

Amazon expanded Creator Connections in 2025 to give brands a structured way to
work with creators. The brief is what closes the deal. weak briefs get ignored,
clear briefs get accepted. This skill writes them.

## When to use this

- A brand is enrolled in Creator Connections and has not run a campaign.
- A specific creator is being approached for a paid post.
- Building a 5-10 creator roster for a launch.
- TikTok Shop influencer collaboration in parallel to Amazon.

## The framework. The Brief Components

A creator brief that gets accepted has four sections.

1. **Product fit summary.** One paragraph on what the product is, who the buyer is,
   and why this creator's audience matches. specific to the creator.

2. **The ask.** Format (Reel, TikTok, YouTube short, post), platform, length, key
   message points, and what the creator should NOT do (avoid these claims, do not
   compare to competitor X).

3. **The deal.** Commission tier or flat rate, plus any product-gift element. Tie
   to attributed sales where possible (see amz-attribution-campaign-planner).

4. **The logistics.** Product ship-by date, content due date, posting schedule,
   approval flow, attribution link.

## Step by step

1. **Collect inputs.** Brand, product, target buyer, creator's name, channel, and
   typical audience.

2. **Score the fit.** Audience overlap, content style match, past brand partnerships.
   Skip creators who do not fit. one wrong-fit post does nothing.

3. **Write the brief.** Four sections above. brief is one-page max. creators do not
   read longer.

4. **Set the deal.** Commission via Creator Connections or TikTok Shop affiliate.
   Flat-rate for top creators. Hybrid for the middle.

5. **Write the outreach DM.** 2-3 sentences. introduce, state the fit, link to a
   one-pager brief. do not paste the brief into the DM.

6. **Plan the relationship.** A single post is rarely worth the effort. plan a
   2-3 post series with the creator for compounding effect.

7. **Run the quality check**, then deliver.

## Output format

```
## Creator Brief. [creator]

### Fit summary
[one paragraph: product, buyer, why this creator]

### The ask
Format: [Reel/TikTok/YT short/post]   Length: [seconds]
Key message points: [list]
Avoid: [list]

### The deal
Commission: [% or flat]   Product gift: [yes/no]
Attribution link: [tag pattern]

### Logistics
Product ship-by: [date]   Content due: [date]   Posting: [date]
Approval: [yes/no, lead time]

### Outreach DM template
[2-3 sentence opener with brief link]

### Relationship plan
[multi-post sequence if applicable]
```

## Worked example

Brand: skincare line. Creator: a micro-influencer in the sensitive-skin niche, 80k
followers, average 3% engagement. Fit: high. her audience is asking the same
questions the product answers. Ask: one TikTok video, 30-60s, leading with the
sensitive-skin angle, avoiding any medical claims. Deal: 10% commission on
attributed sales via Creator Connections, plus 60 USD product gift. Outreach DM:
"Hi [name], your sensitive-skin content is exactly the audience our serum is built
for. We are running a 10% commission program through Amazon Creator Connections.
Would you be open to a one-page brief?" Multi-post plan: if the first lands well,
2 more videos at increasing commission tiers over the next 8 weeks.

## Quality check

- Creator fit is scored, not assumed.
- The brief is one page max. creators do not read longer.
- The 'avoid' list is included so the creator doesn't accidentally produce
  unusable content.
- The deal uses attribution (where possible) to tie commission to real outcomes.
- A multi-post relationship is planned, not just a one-off.

## Common mistakes

- **Generic outreach.** A copy-paste DM with no creator-specific fit reference.
- **No 'avoid' list.** A creator's first instinct may be a comparison or a medical
  claim that the brand cannot use.
- **Flat-rate everything.** Paying upfront for a creator who never converts is
  expensive. Commission via Creator Connections aligns incentives.
- **One-and-done.** A single post rarely justifies the work. multi-post
  relationships compound.
